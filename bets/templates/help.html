Help

The figures

Realised profit
Everything settled bets, rewards and adjustments have made or lost. Stakes count once their bet settles. Deposits and withdrawals never count.
Still to come
What you should still make: the worse outcome of each open position, free bets you hold valued at your retention, and rewards you've qualified for but not received.
Retention
The share of a free bet's face value you actually kept, from your own settled free-bet positions (a typical figure is used until you've settled three).
Available to bet
Bookmaker balances plus exchange balances, minus the lay liability the exchanges are holding.
Lay liability held
What an exchange holds back while a lay is open: what you'd pay if the selection wins. It isn't spent; it comes back when the lay settles.
Stakes in play
Cash back stakes at bookmakers on bets that haven't settled yet.
Temporary funding
Money moved from your bank that has to go back. Returns repay the oldest first; anything returned above what's outstanding counts as a bankroll withdrawal.
Your capital
Money that is yours in the bankroll: opening balances and bankroll contributions, less bankroll withdrawals.
Reconciliation
The difference between the app's balance and the real one when you check a balance. It's recorded openly and counts in profit or loss.
SNR / SR
Free bet with stake not returned (the usual kind) / stake returned.
Qualifying loss
The small loss you accept on the bet that unlocks an offer's reward.

Statuses

Offer
Not started → Qualifying (a bet is recorded) → Awaiting reward (the qualifier settled) → Reward received (free bets added) → Converting (one is used) → Complete (none left to use). The app moves offers on by itself; you can still set the status by hand.
Position
Open until every bet in it is settled; Incomplete while it has a back but no lay (or the other way round); Void when every bet was voided.
Bookmaker standing
Fine, Restricted (gubbed: stakes limited or promotions removed) or Closed. Restricted accounts are flagged when you record a bet there.

Fixing things

  • Undo: after settling, adding free bets or a reward, a transfer, an entry or balance adjustments, the message at the top has an Undo button for 30 minutes.
  • Issues: the Issues page lists everything that needs putting right, each with a link to fix it.
  • Wrong position: on the position page, a bet can be moved to another position or a new one, and two positions can be merged.
  • Balances: Accounts → Check balances, then tick the ones that match or type the real balance.
  • Nothing is ever deleted: changes supersede the old ledger entries, which stay in the history and the audit trail.
