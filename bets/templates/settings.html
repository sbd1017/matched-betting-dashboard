Trusted Assistant mode (automatic import)

When ON, entries the Matched Betting Assistant submits for the ticked types are confirmed automatically instead of waiting under Imports — only if they pass the same checks as Confirm and are complete and unremarkable. Every automatic confirmation is recorded in the audit trail. It records only what you already did; it never places bets.
